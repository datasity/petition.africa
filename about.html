Guidelines, privacy and data

How Petition.africa works.

Community guidelines

Petitions must ask a person or institution with real power to make a specific change. We remove petitions that incite violence or hatred, target or expose private individuals, share someone's phone number or address, or make claims that are clearly false.

New petitions are checked automatically. Anything that looks risky is held for review, and petitions reported by several people are hidden until our team checks them.

Signing

You sign in with your phone number and a one-time code. Each phone number can sign a petition once. Your number is never shown. You choose whether your first name and area appear on the petition.

Leaders

Heads of state for all 54 countries, and governors, premiers and regional ministers for Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana and South Africa, were checked on . Other offices show their title until a name is verified. Anyone can suggest a correction, and we check each one before publishing it.

Boundaries come from geoBoundaries (William & Mary geoLab) under open licences that require attribution. They are shown for navigation only and imply no position on any disputed border.

Prototype status

This version stores data in your browser, sends no real SMS codes, and includes demonstration petitions and signature counts. A live launch needs a server database, an SMS provider, rate limiting, a moderation team and data protection registration in each country of operation.
